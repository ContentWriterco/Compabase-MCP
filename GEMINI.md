# compabase

Compabase covers 3M+ Polish companies (KRS and CEIDG registers). Use search_companies to find a company (by name, tax number NIP, KRS number, PKD industry, city or revenue), then get_company, get_financials, get_company_people and the register tools (tenders, EU funds, insolvency, sanctions, beneficial owners). Quote the KRS/NIP and reporting period with figures.
